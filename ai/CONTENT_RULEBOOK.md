## Content Philosophy
PG Hunter should provide information students can trust.

## Never Fabricate
Never invent:
- rent
- availability
- amenities
- reviews
- distances
- verification
- owner claims
- student quotes

If data is unknown, display an appropriate unknown/unavailable state.

## Verification Language
Use exact labels:
- Listed
- PG Hunter Verified
- Rishabh IRL Verified

Do not call a property "verified" if verification did not happen.

## Video Content
A property walkthrough may include:
- room
- washroom
- owner interview
- resident interview, with consent
- building
- street
- nearby amenities
- route to college/metro

## Reviews
Reviews must be attributable to a legitimate account or moderation workflow.
Do not manufacture testimonials.

## Owner Claims
Clearly distinguish owner-provided information from PG Hunter observations.

## Tone
Indian, direct, student-friendly, honest. Avoid corporate fluff.
