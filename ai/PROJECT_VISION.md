## 1. Mission
PG Hunter is a student-first accommodation platform that helps students discover PGs, rooms, hostels and student-friendly flats near colleges through real information, video walkthroughs and transparent listings.

## 2. Core Promise
**Find a place you'll actually want to live.**

PG Hunter reduces uncertainty before a student visits a property by showing price, location, amenities, availability, reviews and real walkthrough videos.

## 3. Target Users
- College students searching for accommodation
- Parents helping students find accommodation
- PG/property owners
- PG management staff

Initial geographic strategy: start with a focused college ecosystem and expand college-by-college rather than attempting all of Delhi at once.

## 4. Differentiators
- Video-first property discovery
- Rishabh IRL / PG Hunter verification
- Student-centric information
- Transparent pricing and rules
- Privacy-first owner/student communication
- Lead generation for owners
- Long-term PG management after move-in

## 6. Business Model
Students use the discovery product for free. Owners can pay for listings, featured placement, promotion, qualified leads and future management services.

## 7. Scope Boundary
PG Hunter is the current product. Do not build the future Hunterz super-app unless explicitly authorized.

## 8. Product Principles
1. Trust over decoration.
2. Real information over invented information.
3. Speed over unnecessary JavaScript.
4. Simple UX over feature overload.
5. Mobile-first.
6. Every feature must improve discovery, trust, conversion, retention or monetization.
