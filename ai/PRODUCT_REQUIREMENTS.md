# Cloudflare Architecture Decisions â€” MVP Scope Addendum

## Initial Development Scope

The first development phase is intentionally limited to:

1. Homepage
2. PG/property listings
3. Search and filtering
4. College/locality discovery
5. Property detail pages
6. Property photos
7. YouTube walkthrough videos
8. Verification indicators
9. Student enquiry flow
10. Admin listing management
11. Basic owner management
12. Basic lead management

## Explicitly Deferred

- Full PG management
- Private student-owner messaging
- Bills and rent notifications
- Maintenance requests
- File/document exchange
- AI Matchmaker
- Semantic search
- Advanced market intelligence
- Super-app features

These may be designed for future compatibility but must not block MVP development.

## Product Principle

PG Hunter should first prove that students will discover properties and that property owners will receive valuable leads.

Build the discovery and lead-generation loop before expanding into a complete PG operating system.
